CodeLens is a next-generation code quality analyzer that inspects source code for critical errors, architectural smells, security gaps, and coding standards. Using customized local static models combined with advanced AI models like Qwen, CodeLens analyzes your input classes or files, generates a comprehensive health score, and provides actionable code suggestions to make your software safer and cleaner.
Our health scoring algorithm calculates quality on a scale of 0 to 100. It begins at 100 and subtracts penalties based on the severity of issues detected:
  • 🐛 Critical Errors: Significant penalty (-10 points per error) due to potential runtime crashes.
  • 🌿 Code Smells: Moderate penalty (-4 points per smell) focusing on clean code principles.
  • 📦 OOP Violations: Minor structural penalties that impact future maintainability.
Scores above 85 are marked as **Great** (Green), between 65 and 84 as **Good** (Blue), between 45 and 64 as **Fair** (Yellow), and below 45 as **Poor** (Red).
CodeLens prioritizes high-performance, developer-focused AI models:
  • Free Plan: Access to core static analyzers and basic model parameters to test features.
  • Pro Plan: Advanced multi-model metrics with higher daily analysis counts.
  • Ultra Plan: Exclusive access to high-fidelity models like **Qwen** with unlimited code refactoring limits and instant rendering.
Daily analysis limits are automatically reset 24 hours after your last usage, rather than on a fixed midnight timer. This gives you a continuous, rolling window to schedule your testing schedules flexibly. You can review your real-time limit status on the **Usage Limits** tab in the sidebar at any time.

1. Security Gaps and Code Scanning Guarantees

We believe your intellectual property belongs exclusively to you. Any source code, scripts, or repositories uploaded or scanned within CodeLens are analyzed inside temporary server sandboxes. We do not store, distribute, or use your uploaded code to train commercial or third-party AI models. Once an analysis is finished, details are cached in your local session storage and in your personal history table, which is fully encrypted in transit and at rest.

2. OAuth and Avatar Integration

When logging in using verified third-party providers (such as Google or GitHub), CodeLens requests minimal public profile scopes to authenticate your session safely. This includes your email, first name, last name, and profile picture avatar. Avatar pictures are formatted as a circular badge inside our top headers and sidebar widgets, helping you maintain a seamless premium developer dashboard experience without manual uploads.

3. Data Deletion and Portability

You have full control over your footprint on CodeLens. You can clear your analysis history globally with a single click, or permanently delete your entire account along with all associated models, star states, subscriptions, and profile data from the Account Settings panel. Once deleted, this information is wiped from our main databases and backup nodes within 24 hours.
