How does CodeLens analyze my Python code?
CodeLens utilizes a hybrid engine. First, our parser compiles your code into an Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) to verify structural correctness. Second, custom heuristic rules analyze complexity and OOP hierarchies. Finally, our LLM Surgeon refactors the code, explaining it clearly to help both professional developers and students master best practices.

Absolutely. We take privacy extremely seriously. Your raw code blocks are analyzed on-the-fly and are never persisted to long-term storage unless you choose to save them to your analysis history. You also retain full capability to permanently delete any history record instantly from your dashboard.
